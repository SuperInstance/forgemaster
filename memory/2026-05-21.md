### Scientific Experiments (6 dispatched, 5 completed, 1 failed)

#### ✅ Experiment 1: Eisenstein Quantization
- **Hypothesis**: Hexagonal lattice is provably optimal 2D quantizer
- **Result**: 3.9% MSE advantage, 15.5% packing density advantage (Thue's theorem confirmed)
- **Nuance**: Packing advantage means fewer codebook entries, not lower MSE directly
- **Error distribution**: Eisenstein concentrates more errors in small-magnitude bins (9.5% vs 8.0% under 0.02)
- **Trade-off**: Slightly higher max error (elongated Voronoi cells) for better average-case
- **Files**: experiments/eisenstein-quantization/

#### ✅ Experiment 2: Holonomy Convergence
- **Hypothesis**: Ring averaging converges to zero holonomy in O(diameter) rounds
- **Result**: CONFIRMED. Laman topology is the sweet spot.
- **Key data**: N=20: Ring=604 rounds, Laman=82 rounds (8× faster, only 37 vs 20 edges), Complete=1 round (190 edges)
- **Byzantine finding**: Causes FALSE CONSENSUS — agents converge to attacker's value. Safety failure, not liveness.
- **Majority voting (median) FAILS** on ring topology — ring averaging is strictly superior
- **Magnitude-independent**: 1° and 90° initial disagreement converge similarly
- **Files**: experiments/holonomy-convergence/

#### ✅ Experiment 3: Deadband SNR
- **Hypothesis**: Deadband beats moving average for sparse signals
- **Result**: CONFIRMED, with important nuance
- **Sparse signals**: Deadband 89% correlation, MA 39%. MA actually DEGRADES SNR by 5.6 dB
- **Dense signals**: MA 96% correlation, Deadband 82%. MA is strictly better
- **Key insight**: Deadband is NOT a low-pass filter. It exploits temporal sparsity, MA exploits frequency content
- **Suppression rate tracks erf(τ/(σ√2))** with 0.065 mean error
- **Files**: experiments/deadband-snr/

#### ✅ Experiment 4: COLLECT→SELECT→COMPILE Universality
- **Hypothesis**: Every pipeline decomposes to C→S→C, threshold θ is the control parameter
- **Result**: CONFIRMED. 141 regime transitions across 5 ecosystems.
- **Transition points**: flux θ≈0.24, fleet θ≈0.45, sunset θ≈0.21, constraint θ≈55, compression θ≈0.25
- **Mathematical argument**: Sufficiency (θ uniquely determines output), Necessity (1D decision boundary = threshold)
- **Analogous to phase transitions** in statistical mechanics
- **Files**: experiments/collect-select-compile/

#### ✅ Experiment 5: Pythagorean48 Encoding
- **Hypothesis**: Pythagorean triples give zero-drift rotations
- **Result**: CONFIRMED. 52 triples (not 48) → 128 unique directions via 8-way symmetry
- **Zero drift**: magnitude² = 1.000000000000000 after 1000 chained rotations (exact Fraction arithmetic)
- **Float32 comparison**: magnitude² = 1.0000172 after same chain, drift growing linearly
- **MSE**: Pyth128 = 5.71°², better than 36-dir (8.29°²) and 16-dir (41.99°²)
- **Files**: experiments/pythagorean48-encoding/
